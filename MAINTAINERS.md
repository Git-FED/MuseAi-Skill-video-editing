## Responsibilities

- Review and merge PRs.
- Maintain skill versions and manifest hashes.
- Maintain the static site and its Pages deployment.
- Respond to security reports within 72 hours.
- Keep the shared skill-folder contract intact.
- When a skill is extracted into its own repository, configure its release
  workflow secret and protect the Minisign private key.
- Publish the matching trusted public key and consumer verification guidance
  before asking anyone to rely on signed releases.

The per-skill release workflows are templates nested in this umbrella repository;
they are not active here and publish a signed manifest, not a skill ZIP.

## Becoming a maintainer

Sustained, high-quality contributions that follow the contract. Nominated by an
existing maintainer, approved by the current maintainer.
